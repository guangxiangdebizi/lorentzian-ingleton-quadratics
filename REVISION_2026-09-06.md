# Explicit Clifford-bound revision -- 2026-09-06

The main lower bound is now **1/16384**, with **1/16** for pairwise
disjoint queries. The exact optimized upper family remains
`(-107+51 sqrt(17))/128`; no sharpness or global-priority claim is added.

The paper contains its own complete finite Schur/Fischer proof of the
PSD bound `128^(-d)` and its own real Clifford determinant-power
construction. Neither depends on the old quadratic theorem, avoiding a
circular proof. The rank-zero case is padded to a two-dimensional
representation, and zero rows/noncommuting atoms are included.

The old MST bound `476656^(-5)` and the degeneration proof remain as
alternatives, with the HHSW/BHKL attribution correction preserved. They
no longer serve as the proof of the new main numerical bound.

## Artifacts

- `src/gaussian_floor.py`: exact auxiliary-covariance and Schur identities,
  including a known ratio strictly below one.
- `src/clifford_floor.py`: real exterior generators, complete low-rank
  multivariate polynomial identities, 17 constructions and 102 regularized
  cases, plus signed-generator and Hessian negative controls.
- `src/reproduce.py`: schema v4, new numerical theorem fields, full source
  provenance, and both new audit reports. Old results are legacy fields.
- `tests/test_gaussian_clifford.py`: six new gates, bringing the total to 18.

The new checker sources are adapted from the offline research round in
the companion research-map repository. Adaptations are limited to local
proof-path wording and JSON-native list serialization; mathematical
functions and exact tests are preserved. Their current bytes are bound
by the provenance report.

```powershell
python -B src/reproduce.py --check reports/certificates.json
python -B -m unittest discover -s tests -v
```

Both commands are for validation, not regeneration. To intentionally
regenerate after a source change, use `--output reports/certificates.json`
first and wait for it to complete before running tests.

The local PDF has 16 pages and no final typesetting warnings other than
the compiler's nonfatal Fontconfig configuration notice. The repository
continues to exclude generated PDFs from Git; the source and CI build
remain the published artifacts. The three authors and shared Shanghai
University of Electric Power affiliation are unchanged.
